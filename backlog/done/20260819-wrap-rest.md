# Close the wrap class: range fences on allocation, union harmonisation and sum accumulation

Goal: the machine-integer fence of adversarial round 1 becomes closing
instead of "most of it". The three recorded unfenced surfaces in
`backends/clingo/lib/translate.ml` get the same `viol/2` range guards
the expression translation carries: the allocation recurrence (the
pot×weight product, the weight sum and the share sum), the
uniqueness-union scale harmonisation (the per-position compile-time
upscale), and the `#sum` accumulations of the aggregation translation
(contributor bound plus contributor count bound, so the running sum
provably stays inside 2^31). `#count` aggregates need no fence (a count
cannot reach 2^31 before grounding explodes) — recorded, not guarded.

then genuinely out of reach, or disappears.
