Design forced by the material: the full casus raises exactly ONE resolver
ERROR — the §4.1 datum+datum quirk in `toka-23` (`bevestigingsinterval`) —
which `CasusTest` itself pins as the single tolerated diagnostic, and

casus MINUS `toka-23`; the gate holds that fragment out by name (a
reason-carrying holdout mirroring gate 7's survivor list) and fails if the
holdout goes stale. Per-fragment dumps were rejected as unworkable: 5
fragments do not resolve standalone and the rest emit semantically-empty
IR with dangling references — only the whole-casus concatenation is a
complete model.
