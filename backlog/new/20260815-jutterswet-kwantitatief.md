# Jutterswet: the quantitative extension articles

Goal: author the statute articles that give the island its quantitative
machinery (the roadmap table in `corpus/jutterswet/README.md`):
penningbedrag and vondstdrempel as parameters revised yearly by the
eilandraad (vintages), vondst-aanmelding as fact creation with
aggregation ("het aantal vondsten … in het penningjaar"), the gezamenlijke
berging with allocation in all modalities (naar rato, maximum, afronding,
onverdeelde rest), units (kg, €), stormvloeddag as day kind (double
count), uniqueness/consistency of vondstnummers, and the hardheidstoets
as decision-table material — as dated amendment texts consistent with the
existing version history.

Acceptance: new consolidated wet version(s) + amendment texts in
`corpus/jutterswet/`, self-contained (no course references, no private
repos), internally consistent with `versiegeschiedenis.md` dates and
`dossiers.md`; README roadmap updated to point at the delivered articles;
gate passes.

May touch: `corpus/jutterswet/`, `backlog/`.

Out of scope: RegelSpraak sources or IR files for these articles (golden
corpus work, needs the emitter); the zorgtoeslag case.
