# datumsetbron-totaalvouw

`datumsetbron-somvouw`'s twin under the other additive kind. Same
document, same case, same two executors' refusals; the wire kind is
`TOTAAL` instead of `SOM`, and the pinned ASP sentence names it.

It is a case of its own rather than a second case file because the two
kinds part on exactly one clause — the empty set, where §5.8.2 gives the
som its `emptyFallback` and §4.20 gives the totaal an unconditional 0 — and
a witness that pins one kind's sentence says nothing about the other's.
Both executors carry the two kinds in ONE arm, so a mutation that removes
the refusal removes both; that is a property of the implementation and
not a reason to pin only half of it.

The additive fold is where this card's measured asymmetry sits, and
there are TWO of them, not one. They share a cause: `agg_som` and
`agg_totaal` fold with `List.fold_left add first rest`, so with fewer
than TWO filled contributors no addition is ever performed and `ref`
answers without meeting the type error. The ASP backend declines the
form whatever the case supplies, because the refusal is about the
DOCUMENT and not about which members a particular case happens to link.
So below two contributors the two executors part, and the direction is
the harmless one: a refusal against an answer no chapter defines.

| bijdragers | `ref` (som) | `ref` (totaal) | clingo |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | leeg | **0** (§4.20) | weigert |
| 1 | dat ene lid, een DATUM | dat ene lid, een DATUM | weigert |
| ≥ 2 | FOUT `plus: expected a number` | idem | weigert |

The SINGLETON row is the sharper one, and it is why the register text
says "from two contributors onward": at one member the som gives back a
DATE, which is a well-typed answer — the fold is an identity there —
while at zero the totaal gives back a NUMBER in a date slot, which is
not. Both are measured (2026-09-20); the singleton row is pinned
executably in the ASP backend's own suite (`ref` answering beside the
translation declining, on one document and one case), and the empty
TOTAAL row is NOT, for a reason worth stating: the value `ref` computes
there cannot be WRITTEN DOWN as an expectation at all. The case reader
rejects `{"number": "0"}` against a `Datum in dagen` declaration —
`value variant "number" does not match the declared datatype` — so there
is no pin to write. That the outcome is inexpressible says more than a
pin would have.

Nothing in the corpus carries the shape and no producer writes it (the
frontend types the sentence out). Correcting `ref`'s 0 is the arbiter's
own business and out of this card's reach.
