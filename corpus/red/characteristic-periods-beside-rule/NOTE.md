# characteristic-periods-beside-rule — true periods of the case beside the rule that writes them

An Afdracht is rentedragend from its vervaldatum to its betaaldatum: a rule
writes the time-dependent characteristic within a window (RUNTIME-SEMANTICS
ch. 3.10, 3.11). The case also gives A1 the true periods [2025-01-01,
2025-02-01) of rentedragend. A case-supplied timeline of a characteristic a
rule writes is not defined (ch. 3.12, row 7): the session faults with EF-041,
characteristic `rentedragend`, label `A1`, in the reference interpreter, the
ASP backend and both branches of the Drools backend. Read as one more writer,
the input would either stand beside the rule's periods or be replaced by them,
and nothing in the specification chooses.
