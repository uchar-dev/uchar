# fractional-power-negative-base — a negative base under a fractional exponent

The wortelmaat of a Vondst is its gewicht to the power 0,5, rounded down to 2
decimals. V1's gewicht is −4. A negative base has no real power under any
fractional exponent, with or without a rounding (RUNTIME-SEMANTICS ch. 2.2):
the session faults with EF-044 in the reference interpreter and both branches
of the Drools backend. The ASP backend translates a whole non-negative
literal exponent only and refuses the document with CLINGO-041 (gate 8's
divergence list).
