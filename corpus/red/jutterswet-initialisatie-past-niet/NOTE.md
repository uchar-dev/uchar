# jutterswet-initialisatie-past-niet — a non-exact value of an initialisation that decides

The flank of `corpus/golden/jutterswet-initialisatie-naast-regel`: V6 not gemeld,
opbrengst 100, deelnemers 3. The initialisation decides, and 100/3 does not fit
the whole `bergloon` (EF-006, RUNTIME-SEMANTICS ch. 2.4, 5.7). The rules stand in the order of the casus; `jutterswet-initialisatie-past-niet-omgekeerd` has them in the reverse order, with the same expectations.
