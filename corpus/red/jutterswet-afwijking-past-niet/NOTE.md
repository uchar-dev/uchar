# jutterswet-afwijking-past-niet — a non-exact value of a default that is reached

The flank of `corpus/golden/jutterswet-deling-in-afwijking`, case 6: V6 not
gemerkt, geschat 30, grond 100. The deviation of `toeslag` decides, and 100/30
plus 5,00 does not fit its two decimals (EF-006, RUNTIME-SEMANTICS ch. 2.4, 5.7). The rules stand in the order of the casus; `jutterswet-afwijking-past-niet-omgekeerd` has them in the reverse order, with the same expectations.
