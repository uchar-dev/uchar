# own-total-full-timeline — the subject's own total over a full timeline of the case

The jaarloon of a Jutter is the total of his wachtloon, a monthly attribute,
without a clip: the subject's own temporal total, which reads the
asOfDate-bucket scalar, the value of the month that holds the evaluation date
(RUNTIME-SEMANTICS ch. 3.6, 8.4). The case gives J1's wachtloon as a full
timeline of 7 over 2024 (asOfDate 2024-06-15). An executor that needs the one
convention and finds the other faults: the session faults with EF-042,
attribute `wachtloon`, label `J1`, in the reference interpreter, the ASP
backend and both branches of the Drools backend. Totalled over the whole
timeline the jaarloon would be 84; read as the bucket of June, 7.
