# jutterswet-sluitdag-gemeld — defaults whose consequence folds over a role

A Berging holds vondsten, and a Kustwacht beheert bergingen. Four defaults
groups, written as `Afwijking van` and `Uitzondering op` trees, and two
generated by the opgave marker (`(mag opgegeven worden)`, the RegelSpraak
frontend's grammar/DIVERGENCES.md §7), decide a date or a weight whose
consequence folds over the vondsten:

| target | the fold | where it stands |
|---|---|---|
| sluitdag of a Berging | de laatste van de bergdag van alle vondsten van de Berging | the base, under "gesteld op zijn gemelde sluitdag indien zijn gemelde sluitdag gevuld is" |
| uiterste meldingsdag (marked) | (de laatste van de meldingsdag van alle vondsten van de Berging) plus 14 dagen | the base of a tree the marker moves to 'berekende uiterste meldingsdag', under "gesteld op zijn sluitdag indien het aantal vondsten van de Berging groter is dan 2" |
| afrekendag (marked) | de laatste van (de laatste van de bergdag …) en (de laatste van de meldingsdag …) | the initialisation the marker lifts into its generated group, below the opgave and 'berekende afrekendag' (the gemelde sluitdag where filled) |
| vrachtgewicht | de som van het gewicht van alle vondsten van de Berging | an exception, "indien zijn gemelde gewicht leeg is", above "gesteld op zijn gemelde gewicht" |
| sluitdag of a Kustwacht | de laatste van de bergdag van alle vondsten van alle bergingen van de Kustwacht | the base, two roles away, under the Kustwacht's gemelde sluitdag |

RUNTIME-SEMANTICS ch. 7.1: a default decides with its consequence where its
justification holds, and a leeg consequence falls through to the next
default. Ch. 1.2e: laatste is an ordering fold over the contributed values;
an empty contributor is skipped and an empty or all-empty source folds to
leeg, as the sum does; a date plus a duration propagates an empty date. The
exception of the uiterste meldingsdag reads the sluitdag, which another group
writes, so its group decides after that one (ch. 7.4). The initialisation of
the afrekendag decides where the afrekendag is empty in the input (ch. 7.3).

## Hand computation

`case1-drie-bergingen.json` (20 expectations). K1 beheert B1 and B2; K2
beheert B3 and has a gemelde sluitdag 25-03-2026; K3 beheert no berging. B1
has V1 (bergdag 09-03-2026, meldingsdag 05-03-2026, gewicht 4), V2
(03-03-2026, 11-03-2026, 7) and V3 (no bergdag, 06-03-2026, 2), in that
order; B2 has a gemelde sluitdag 12-03-2026, a gemeld gewicht 30 and V4
(01-03-2026, 02-03-2026, 5); B3 has no vondst.

- sluitdag: B1 has no gemelde sluitdag, so the base decides: the latest of
  {09-03, 03-03} (V3 is skipped) = **09-03-2026**. The first member holds the
  latest date and the last filled member the earliest, so a positional
  reading fails. B2 **12-03-2026**, the gemelde. B3: the fold over no
  contributor is leeg, the base falls through and nothing decides: **leeg**.
- uiterste meldingsdag: B1 has three vondsten, so the afwijking decides with
  its sluitdag: berekende **09-03-2026**, and without an opgave the
  uiterste meldingsdag **09-03-2026**. A group that decided before the
  sluitdag was written would read it empty, fall through to the base and
  answer 11-03-2026 + 14 = 25-03-2026. B2 has one vondst, so the base: the
  latest meldingsdag 02-03-2026 + 14 dagen = **16-03-2026**, twice. B3: leeg
  + 14 dagen is leeg, the base falls through: **leeg**, twice.
- afrekendag: 'berekende afrekendag' is the gemelde sluitdag where filled:
  B1 **leeg**, B2 **12-03-2026**. B1 has neither an opgave nor a berekende
  value, so the initialisation decides: the latest of 09-03 (the latest
  bergdag) and 11-03 (the latest meldingsdag) = **11-03-2026**. B2
  **12-03-2026**, the berekende value above the initialisation. B3: the
  latest of two empty folds is leeg: **leeg**.
- vrachtgewicht: B1 has no gemeld gewicht, so the exception: 4 + 7 + 2 =
  **13**. B2 **30**, the gemelde. B3: the sum over no contributor is leeg,
  the exception falls through to the base, whose gemeld gewicht is empty:
  **leeg**.
- sluitdag of a Kustwacht: K1 over the vondsten of B1 and B2, {09-03, 03-03,
  01-03} = **09-03-2026**; K2 **25-03-2026**, the gemelde; K3 **leeg**.

`case2-opgave-en-een-lege-bijdrager.json` (12 expectations). K4 beheert B4
and B5. B4 has an opgegeven uiterste meldingsdag 30-03-2026, an opgegeven
afrekendag 01-04-2026 and no vondst. B5 has a gemelde sluitdag 20-03-2026 and
V6 (no bergdag, meldingsdag or gewicht), V7 (14-03-2026, 13-03-2026, 6) and
V8 (02-03-2026, 04-03-2026, 1).

- sluitdag: B4 **leeg**; B5 **20-03-2026**, the gemelde.
- uiterste meldingsdag: B4 has no vondst, so the base, leeg: berekende
  **leeg**, and the opgave decides: **30-03-2026**. B5 has three vondsten
  (V6 counts, empty as it is), so the afwijking with its sluitdag: berekende
  **20-03-2026**, and **20-03-2026**.
- afrekendag: B4 berekende **leeg**, the opgave **01-04-2026**. B5
  **20-03-2026**, the berekende value; the initialisation below it would
  give the latest of 14-03 and 13-03.
- vrachtgewicht: B4 **leeg**, as B3. B5: 6 + 1 (V6 is skipped) = **7**.
- sluitdag of K4: over the vondsten of B4 and B5, {14-03, 02-03} =
  **14-03-2026**.

Where an opgave is given, the berekende value is empty, so the generated
characteristic 'afwijkende opgave' is not attributed and the generated
consistencies hold. A no-op run fails both cases: it leaves every derived
value leeg (case 1: 13 mismatches, case 2: 8).

## The square

`ref` and the ASP backend replay both cases (`oracle: PASS (20
expectations)`, `(12 expectations)`; clingo the same). The exec-model branch
plays them, `PLAYED 2`; the DRL-text branch refuses the document by name
(`drl: ch. 7 prioritizedDefaults — the DRL-text branch has no defaults
lowering`).

Measured on the exec-model branch before the card
drools-exec-defaults-consequentie-vouw (2026-09-30):

    EXCLUDE transpiler SKIP: defaults: defaults A:Kustwacht.sluitdag — defaults (Kustwacht.sluitdag): niet-reduceerbare consequentie (far-side/aggregatie): <default>

The branch refused every group whose consequence folds. It now lowers such a
fold as it lowers one in a rule's value: an accumulate on the group's rule,
whose result the consequence reads.

Not in this golden, with its reason: the minimum or maximum of two number
folds (the ASP backend does not translate a listed fold whose operands are
folds over numbers, `TRANSLATION FAULT: expression kind not translated`, in
an ordinary rule too), and a fold over a relation whose two roles are both
plural (the exec-model branch refuses it by name, as it does in a rule's
value). Both are rows of `DefaultsConsequentieVouwWitnessTest` in the Drools
backend: the first with the values of `ref`, the second as a refusal that
names the form.
